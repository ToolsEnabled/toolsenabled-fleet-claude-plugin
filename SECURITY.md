# Security policy

## Report a problem

Please report a security problem privately, not in a public issue:

- **Report a vulnerability** on this repository's Security tab (GitHub's private reporting), or
- email **security@toolsenabled.ai**.

Reports from anyone are welcome, including from Anthropic as the directory that lists this plugin. Tell us the Fleet
version (Claude Code's `/plugin` list shows it), what you found, and how to reproduce it. We read
every report, reply as soon as we can, keep you informed while we fix it, tell you when the fix is released, and credit
you in the release notes if you want that.

The latest release is the supported one; fixes ship as a new release.

## Safe harbor

We want people to look for problems in Fleet, and we will not treat good-faith research as an attack.

If you research and report in good faith under this policy:

- we consider your research authorized, and we will not bring or support any legal action against you for it,
  including under anti-hacking, anti-circumvention or similar laws;
- we waive any part of our own terms that would otherwise stop you from doing that research;
- if someone else takes legal action against you for research that followed this policy, we will make it known that
  your research was authorized by us.

Good faith means:

- you test Fleet on computers, accounts and projects that are yours or that you have permission to use;
- you do not access, change or keep other people's data beyond what you need to show the problem, and you delete it
  afterwards;
- you do not disrupt services or other people;
- you give us a reasonable chance to fix the problem before you publish it. We will not ask you to stay silent
  indefinitely; if we have not fixed it within 90 days of your report, you may publish.

If you are unsure whether something is covered, ask us first at the addresses above.

This safe harbor covers Fleet's own code and releases. It cannot grant permission for other parties' systems: report a
problem in Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode or a model provider to its own maker.

## Rewards

ToolsEnabled is a small, self-funded company. We do not pay rewards for reports. We are grateful for every one, and we
will credit you publicly if you want that.
